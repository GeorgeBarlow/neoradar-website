# NeoRadar Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

This policy explains what personal data NeoRadar ("we", "us") processes when you sign in with
VATSIM Connect, and why. NeoRadar is a non-commercial project for the VATSIM network.

## 1. Who is responsible

The data controller is the **NeoRadar team**.
Contact: **privacy@neoradar.app**

## 2. What data we collect

When you sign in with VATSIM Connect, VATSIM shares the following with us, and only with your consent
on the VATSIM sign-in screen:

- your **VATSIM CID**
- your **name**
- your **email address**
- your **VATSIM region** (and division / subdivision where provided)

Like any online service, our servers also record technical data when you connect (IP address,
time of the request, and the action requested) in security logs.

We do not collect your VATSIM password; sign-in happens on VATSIM's own pages. We do not sell
your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other websites.

## 3. Why we use it

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Signing you in to NeoRadar Hub and authenticating the NeoRadar client | CID, name, email, region | Performance of the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Letting authorised staff manage their vACC's data packages on the Hub | CID, name, region | Performance of the service (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Keeping an audit log of actions taken on the Hub | CID, name, action and time | Legitimate interest in accountability and integrity of the data (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Keeping the server and infrastructure secure: preventing abuse, investigating incidents | CID, security logs, IP address | Legitimate interest in running a secure service (Art. 6(1)(f)) |

Your email is used only to identify your account and, if needed, to contact you about it. We do not
send newsletters or marketing.

## 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. We use trusted service providers to host,
secure and monitor NeoRadar; they act on our instructions and only process your data to provide
those services to us.

VATSIM is the identity provider; its own handling of your data is covered by the
[VATSIM Privacy Policy](https://vatsim.net/docs/policy/data-protection-and-handling-policy).

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the European Economic Area. When
that happens, we make sure it is protected by an appropriate safeguard recognised under the GDPR, such
as an adequacy decision of the European Commission, Binding Corporate Rules, or the European
Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Account data** (CID, name, email, region): for as long as you have an account. We delete it on
  request, or after 24 months without a sign-in.
- **Security logs**: up to 6 months, unless needed longer to investigate a specific incident.
- **Audit log**: for as long as needed to keep a reliable history of the service, and no longer
  than necessary for that purpose.

## 6. Cookies

The Hub uses a single strictly necessary session cookie to keep you signed in. No analytics or
advertising cookies.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to its use, by
writing to **privacy@neoradar.app**. We reply within one month. You can also remove NeoRadar's access at any
time from your VATSIM account settings.

If you think we have mishandled your data, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

## 8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and post the new version on the Hub.
